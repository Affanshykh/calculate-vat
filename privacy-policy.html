Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Data Collection and Client-Side Processing

At calculate-vat.com, we prioritize user privacy. We do not collect, store, or transmit any tax calculations, invoice values, financial inputs, or business VAT numbers to external servers.

All calculator operations, tax additions/removals, threshold tracking values, and formatting checks occur completely locally inside your web browser using client-side JavaScript.

2. Local Storage

Our calculation log feature uses your browser's local storage to save your recent queries so you can retrieve or compare them easily.

This data is stored purely on your physical device, is not uploaded, and can be cleared instantly by clicking the "Clear All" button in the history log panel or by clearing your browser's site cookies.

3. External Links

We provide external links to official regulatory websites (such as Revenue Commissioners, HM Revenue & Customs, and the EU VIES validation system) to help you submit official tax registrations or check live databases. We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content or privacy practices of these third-party domains.
