About Us

Welcome to calculate-vat.com, a free online tax resource dedicated to helping small businesses, sole traders, freelancers, and international travellers calculate Value-Added Tax (VAT) accurately and efficiently.

Our Mission

Value-Added Tax is one of the most common transaction taxes across Europe, yet calculating additions, removals, and backwards percentages remains a common source of mathematical errors. Our goal is to eliminate that friction by providing fast, clean, and interactive tools that require no technical training or account sign-ups.

Whether you need to strip 23% VAT out of a gross Irish invoice, check whether a partner's VIES number is formatted correctly, estimate tourist refunds, or track your sole trader turnover thresholds, we hope our calculators serve as a valuable reference.

Accurate and Up-to-Date

Our calculations are updated to reflect the latest statutory changes in the Republic of Ireland and the United Kingdom. This includes the July 2026 Irish hospitality rate transitions (bringing restaurants and hairdressers to the 9% tier permanently) and the 9% domestic energy VAT extensions.
